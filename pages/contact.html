Common Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 30-minute delivery promise work?

When you place an order, our system assigns it directly to the nearest kitchen. While the chefs are preparing your dish, a nearby Foodora rider is automatically dispatched so your food is picked up fresh and delivered directly to your doorstep.

What payment methods are supported?

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), JazzCash, Easypaisa, and all major Visa/Mastercard debit and credit cards.

How do I apply promotional discount vouchers?

Visit the Offers page to copy active coupon codes like FOODORA50 or FREEDEL. Paste them in the promo box on your Cart page and click Apply.

Can I cancel my order after placing it?

Yes! You can cancel orders immediately while in "Order Placed" or "Order Confirmed" status directly from the Live Order Tracker page.
