Privacy Policy

Last revised: November 15, 2026

1. Customer Privacy & Local Data Protection

Foodora SaaS places the highest priority on user privacy and data sovereignty. Customer profiles, active carts, order references, and saved addresses are handled locally with strict browser isolation.

2. No External Tracking or Third-Party Analytics

We do not share, sell, or transmit your contact numbers, addresses, or order details to third-party ad brokers or external marketing vendors.

3. Payment Information Security

All payment selections (Cash on Delivery, JazzCash, Easypaisa, Debit/Credit Card) are processed with strict client-side encryption and safety controls.

4. Customer Data Rights

You have full control over your stored delivery addresses, profile details, and preferences. You can update or clear your personal data at any time via your account settings.
